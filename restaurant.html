🇮🇳 A National Health Initiative

Mission Suno India

Eradicate preventable hearing loss across India through early detection, awareness, and accessible care.

🎧 Join the Mission → Claim Free Hearing Test

The Vision

Millions in India suffer in silence from undetected hearing loss. What starts as a minor difficulty can often lead to severe social isolation and cognitive decline. Mission Suno India aims to normalize hearing tests and make quality hearing care accessible to every Indian.

Our Mission

To screen 1 million Indians for hearing loss by 2027, provide affordable hearing solutions, and create awareness about the importance of early intervention through schools, community camps, and digital outreach.
